Section 01 · TR

Altarpieces

Triptychs, or Questions of the Times

Oil, Chinese ink, charcoal, X-rays, collage · 2000-2018

They come from cinematic visions, drawn from my own footage of artists in performance or from situations met on my travels. The triptychs work either as a sequential symbiosis (one subject seen from varying angles) or through affinities of meaning.

About the series

Russian Diary Moods
