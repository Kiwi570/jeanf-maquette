Old emerging artist

A law graduate, archaeologist and art historian, JeanF Jans spent ten years restoring ancient and contemporary works and buildings in Germany, Italy, France and Belgium. In 2000 he decided to devote himself entirely to his work as an artist: an “old emerging artist, or Nomade Polymedia”.

Training and background

  1. °1961

    Born in Antwerp. Lives and works in Brussels.

  2. Studies

    Law (Saint-Louis, Brussels). Degree in archaeology and art history (UCL, Louvain-la-Neuve).

  3. Further training

    History of Palladian architecture, Istituto Andrea Palladio, Vicenza. Conservation and restoration of mural paintings, Florence. Modern art restoration techniques, WZK, Düsseldorf. Ancient masonry and mural painting techniques, San Servolo, Venice. Applied techniques of historic building, Brera, Milan.

  4. 1996-1999

    Ixelles Academy of Fine Arts: sculpture and drawing.

  5. 1990-2000

    Freelance: restoration of ancient and contemporary works, applied techniques on ancient and modern buildings (Germany, Italy, France, Belgium), design and interiors. Guest teacher at the Architects’ College, Córdoba (Argentina).

  6. 2000

    Salzburger Sommerakademie: video, “Ausdruck des Privaten”, with Ellen Cantor. From then on, works full time as an artist.

  7. 2006

    Salzburger Sommerakademie: media workshop with Shirin Neshat and Shoja Azari.

  8. 2009

    Salzburger Sommerakademie: expanded drawing with Lia and Dan Perjovschi.

  9. 2010-2011

    Brussels Playhouse: Meisner technique and directing, with Larry Silverberg and Catherine Zavlav.
